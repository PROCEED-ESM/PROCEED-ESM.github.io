PROCEED Computational Resources

  • NCAR-Wyoming Supercomputing Center (NWSC): PROCEED uses resources from NWSC’s Derecho supercomputing system to run our computationally-intensive PPEs and its Casper supercomputing system to process PPE output and perform analysis.

  • University of Wyoming Advanced Research Computing Center (ARCC): PROCEED uses additional resources from the ARCC MedicineBow Cluster to provide storage that supports running and processing the PPE output.

  • University of Wyoming School of Computing: The School of Computing provides additional support for developing, running, and processing our PPEs.
